Paper for thinking. Digital for sharing.

Testing notes do not need to become documentation while you are still thinking.

Write a question. Cross it out. Draw an arrow. Sketch a state flow. Put two observations next to each other. Paper gets out of the way.

When the information needs to leave your desk, take a photo or scan the page and move it into Jira, Confluence, Slack, Teams or wherever your team works.
